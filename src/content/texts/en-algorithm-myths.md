Every week a new "algorithm hack" goes viral, and every week creators reorganise their entire workflow around it. Most of these hacks are folklore. Here are five of the most common, and what is actually going on underneath.

## Myth 1: Posting time decides everything

Posting time gives you a small early-window advantage, nothing more. A genuinely good piece of content posted at a mediocre time will out-travel a mediocre piece posted at the perfect minute. Optimise the content first; optimise the clock last.

## Myth 2: You must use trending audio

Trending audio helps discovery only when it fits. A trending sound stapled onto an unrelated idea reads as noise and gets skipped, which teaches the system to stop showing you. Fit beats trend.

## Myth 3: Hashtags are dead

Hashtags are not a ranking lever; they are a labelling system. Use a handful of accurate ones so the platform understands the topic, then stop thinking about them.

## Myth 4: Engagement pods work

Coordinated engagement looks exactly like coordinated engagement to a machine built to detect coordination. At best it is neutral, at worst it suppresses you.

## Myth 5: One viral hit changes your life

A single viral video brings a wave of strangers who leave as quickly as they came. Consistency turns a fraction of them into an audience. The hit is the spark; the schedule is the fire.
