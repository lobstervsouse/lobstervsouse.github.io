## The task

Build a repeatable pipeline of authentic creator content to feed both organic and paid channels.

## What I did

Sourced and briefed 20 creators, set up a rights-cleared content library, and built a feedback loop so the best-performing angles got reproduced.

## Result

Over 120 deliverables in one quarter and a 31% drop in cost-per-acquisition on paid social.
