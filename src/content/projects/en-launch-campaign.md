## The task

Take an unknown skincare brand from zero to a credible launch in 30 days, with a content engine the in-house team could run after handover.

## What I did

Built the positioning, a three-pillar content system (educate / show / sell), and a daily calendar across Reels and posts. Filmed and edited the first month, then trained the internal team on the playbook.

## Result

50,000 followers before the first product shipped, and a 4.2% average engagement rate across the launch window.
