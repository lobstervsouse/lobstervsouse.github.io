## The task

Twelve locations were posting in twelve different styles. The goal: one recognisable feed without crushing local personality.

## What I did

Created a flexible template kit, a shared caption voice guide, and a simple approval flow franchisees could follow in minutes.

## Result

Consistent grid across all locations and a 60% lift in profile-to-website clicks.
